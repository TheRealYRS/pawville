## 1. Root causes

| # | Symptom | Root cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Control seems to swap between devices | (a) Players who never opened the wardrobe all defaulted to **Tangerine**, named "Tangerine", and nothing marked your own cat, so two devices showed two identical cats with identical tags. (b) The per-tab presence id lived in `sessionStorage`, which browsers **copy into duplicated tabs**. Two tabs then shared one id: each threw away the other's movement as "its own echo", and both claimed the same presence key. Input was always local (one keyboard listener per TownCanvas mount, cleaned up), so control never actually moved to another cat. | Instance id created **once per page load** in memory (`instanceId.ts`). Un-chosen looks default to your role's coat. Un-named cats get a tag such as "Tangerine ·3f". A cream ring is drawn under **your** cat only. The own-id echo guard stays, so a remote packet can never become or move your cat (tested). |
| 2 | Inviter stays on "Invite pending" after the partner accepts | The inviter never opened the couple channel while the invite was pending (`openTransport` returned early), and **nothing re-read `pv_my_pair` afterwards**, so only a full reload recovered. | The channel opens for pending pairs (the inviter is already a member). The server state is re-read when the partner appears, when the channel (re)connects, on focus, online or tab-visible, and on a bounded schedule while pending (4 s, then 15 s after 2 min, then 60 s after 15 min). |
| 3 | "Online — just you" and "partner away" right after inviting or accepting | (a) **Realtime topic de-duplication.** `client.channel(topic)` returns the *existing* channel if one is still registered. Screen changes, invite/accept (`adopt` closed and re-opened the pair channel even for the same pair) and Strict-Mode remounts created a "new" channel that was really the old one being removed. The old owner's cleanup then removed it from under the new owner, and presence died silently. (b) Remote cats were pruned after 20 s without movement, even while presence still listed them, e.g. a hidden tab or the recipient sitting on the invite page. (c) The town headcount was labelled as if it were partner status, although the recipient leaves the town to open `/pair/CODE`. | `net/channelRegistry.ts`: **one owner per topic.** Owners are serialised, any registered channel for the topic is removed first, and `release()` removes only the channel that owner created. Used by town presence and the pair channel. `adopt` keeps the channel when the pair is unchanged. Present players are never pruned. The town label now reads "N cats **in town**", and partner status comes only from the private couple channel. |
| 4 | Contradictory states (sender pending while recipient accepted; partner "away" after one missed sync) | Separate booleans set by unrelated callbacks. One presence sync without the partner set `partnerOnline=false` immediately, and **cancelled pending activity invitations instantly**. | One controller (`couple/pairController.ts`) holds the canonical state: backend, pair, invite and status, transport, partner presence and session. Every label is **derived** in `couple/coupleView.ts`. The partner counts as "away" only after 6 s of continuous absence, shown as "partner reconnecting…" until then. Activity invitations survive blips and close after the 15 s grace period. |
| 5 | Shared activities don't reliably start on both sides | Activity messages were fire-and-forget. One lost `act:accept` left the host on "Asked …" forever while the guest sat in the lobby. | Each side sends an `act:sync` snapshot every 2 s while a session is active, and immediately when the partner reappears. `reconcile()` applies only legal forward transitions for the **same session id**. Closed session ids are remembered, so late or duplicated messages can't resurrect an old session. |
| 6 | Accepting twice shows "already paired" | No single-flight; a refused accept wasn't checked against the server. | Create and accept are single-flight. An `already_paired` or `invite_closed` refusal is checked with `pv_my_pair`; if you are in fact paired, it reports success. |
| 7 | Demo: "partner away" forever | The demo joiner chose its role with the same picker (default Tangerine). Both tabs became "T", so presence never showed the other role and every message was dropped as coming from the wrong sender. | The creator's role is stored with the code (this browser only); the joiner always gets the **other** role. A same-role tab is reported as a "role clash" instead of failing silently. |
